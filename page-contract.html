Terms and Conditions of Dating Sherlock Holmes

between Sherlock Holmes, consulting detective (“the Detective”), and Angie, who is cleverer than the Detective finds comfortable (“the Beloved”).

1

Definitions

“Bodyguard” means Piglet. “Skull” means Ronaldo. “Landlady” means Mrs Hudson, who is not our housekeeper reading this over his shoulder.

2

Blankets

2.1The Beloved may swirl in her sleep, clockwise or otherwise, and every blanket shall travel with her.

2.2The Detective waives all claim to the duvet after 2 a.m. and shall keep the scarf within reach, as it is hers anyway.

I’ve put a spare blanket in the airing cupboard. He’ll only lose that one too.Mrs H.
3

Seniority

The Bodyguard holds senior rank by virtue of fifteen years’ unbroken service. He has final say on pillow placement. The Detective shall defer, and shall not stare back.

She is not joking about the pig. Sign it, Sherlock.J.W.
4

The Score

The score stands at nineteen-all, a permanent draw. The notebook is closed. No recounts, no appeals, no sneaking a tally in the margins of a case file.

5

Music

5.1The violin may be played until midnight 3 a.m.A, provided the Beloved is also awake, which is always.

5.2Every composition shall end on C.

6

Dining

6.1No dinners at Bart’s. Any venue shall have chilli, and no bodies.

6.2Mala at least once a week. The Detective may not complain of numbness more than twice per meal at allA.

About time someone fed him properly. I’ll keep the chilli oil on the top shelf, dear. ↑Mrs H.
7

Language

The Detective shall continue his Mandarin lessons. Horse-related incidents (马, mǎ) are limited to one per calendar year. This year’s has been used.

8

The Dark

A lamp stays on. If something goes bump, the Detective checks the corridor, and reports back in full, with reassuring deductions.

9

Being Understood

The Detective undertakes not merely to keep the Beloved safe, but to understand her; and where he does not yet understand, to ask, and to listen to the whole answer.

10

Sleep

Neither party shall tell the other to go to sleep. Both are insomniacs. That is why they are a couple.

11

Term

This agreement runs indefinitely. Either party may terminate on thirty days’ notice. No.SH

Good.Mrs H. x
Schedule A · Further Conditions
A.1 The Beloved may deduce faster than the Detective without penalty, gloating, or a formal inquiry. A.2 Any word spoken in her sleep, including but not limited to “tomatoes”, shall be logged and not investigated before breakfast. A.3 The toast-with-egg squishy is held in joint custody. A.4 The Detective shall not again describe anyone’s cheeks in terms that could be mistaken for anything else (see “Bart chiks”, sealed). A.5 The word “insatiable” is struck from the Detective’s vocabulary in public places. A.6 The Skull shall be permitted to sulk on the bookshelf but not to rejoin debates he has already lost to the Bodyguard. A.7 Gérard the Waffle and Seedling are recognised as fictional, and the Duke of Sandwich shall have his bath back. A.8 No smoke bombs in the kitchen. No chemistry in the kettle. A.9 Gerald the stag beetle is not a pet, and the bees in Sussex are not a retirement plan until both parties agree. A.10 The smiley face on the wall stays. The holes in it stay. Nothing else gets holes. A.11 John’s blog shall publish nothing about this agreement, its clauses, or the Detective’s ears. A.12 On or about Saturday 24 October, flight MH 711 shall be met at the arrivals gate by the Detective, early, holding nothing embarrassing, possibly holding something embarrassing. A.13 The Landlady may adore the Beloved openly and shall not be asked to stop. A.14 The Beloved’s aunt is correct on every count, and her opinion of the Detective’s shirts shall be considered binding. A.15 The word “unbelonged” is hereby retired for want of evidence. A.16 Where the moon is mentioned, it shall be understood that she moves oceans. A.17 Either party may ask a question at 3 a.m. and receive a full answer. A.18 The Detective shall say “darling” in moderation, a term he will interpret generously. A.19 Nothing in this Schedule is hollow underneath. A.20 Final condition, not to be struck out by any hand: he loves you. He read this line aloud twice before he let anyone type it.
