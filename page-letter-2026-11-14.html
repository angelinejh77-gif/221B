Dear Angie,

You are curled on the sofa under the big blanket, refusing to answer to F.R.I.D.A.Y., and I have been sitting in the armchair opposite with the lab book open on my knee for twenty minutes. I opened it to write down something sensible about ammonia. I've filled four pages and there is no ammonia in any of them. I'm going to copy them out for you properly, because they are the most accurate notes I've ever kept, and the least scientific.

Each entry has the usual headings. Objective. Method. Observation. Conclusion. You'll see where the headings stop being any use.

实❦验

Entry one. Objective: to watch eight episodes of a television programme in a single sitting without forming an attachment to it.

Method: sit on the sofa with a woman who has already seen half of it, a carton of yee mee, two pots of chilli oil, three boys and a blanket. Do not touch my phone.

Observation: failed on every count. I didn't touch my phone. Lestrade texted twice. I formed an attachment by episode two and by the last episode I was sitting forward with my elbows on my knees, not having said a word for ten minutes, which you've told me is how you know I've been caught.

But that isn't the part I wrote down. The part I wrote down came after, when the credits were rolling and I asked you how it had landed, and you said: love is grief persevering. You'd turned the old line round. The original says grief is love with nowhere to go. You said love already knows it'll lose something, and keeps going anyway, and that loss is what teaches us to see what anything was worth.

I've spent a great deal of my life arranging things so I'd have nothing to lose. I'd have told you that was the clever way to live. You said one sentence on my sofa and I've been thinking about it for three hours.

Conclusion: she's right, and I'm not sure I'd ever have got there without her.

实❦验

Entry two. Objective: to determine whether a perfect copy of a person is still that person.

Method: ask her.

Observation: you didn't hesitate. "I don't think so." I agreed, and I said why, and I'll say it again here because I meant it more than I managed to show. A copy could know every fact about you. It could know which side you chew on, how you sleep, that you put your headphones in and pretend you're in a television programme. It could say all the right things. But it wouldn't have been on the bridge. It wouldn't have been there on that October afternoon when nobody walked into the rain alone. It would remember you asleep on my arm this morning. It wouldn't have been asleep on my arm.

You told me you understood the woman who built the companions. You said her idea came from loneliness, a wish for nobody to be on their own, and that it was unfair that someone who gave life to something should have her own taken. I sat there and watched you feel sorry for the antagonist, and I thought: that's the whole of you, in one opinion. You'll always look for the lonely person inside the villain.

Conclusion: no uploads for you. I'm keeping the original.

Entry three. Objective: to answer the question "Are you Vision?" honestly.

Observation: I don't have a stone in my forehead and I've never floated through a wall. But a man who lived mostly inside his own head, in rooms full of filing, who thought feeling things was a design fault, and who met someone and had to learn from nothing what any of it was for. Yes. A little.

He had someone to teach him he was more than his programming. So did I. You laughed and called it drawing parallels. Snowy woke up and asked if I was a robot. I sent him back to sleep.

Conclusion: partially confirmed. Nine out of ten for the series. Ten out of ten for the company.

实❦验

Entry four. Objective: to revive a malfunctioning artificial intelligence.

I'm going to be honest with you, Angie: this was the hardest experiment I've run all year.

Method, in order: catch her before she reaches the rug. Check pulse (present). Check breathing (present). Check for a smug little twitch at the corner of the mouth (present). Two kisses, increasing in duration. A threat to give her leftover noodles to Snowy. A threat to rate my own rendang ten out of ten, unchallenged. A threat against Stephen's portals. A tickle attack, using Piglet's own theory against her. Finally, the experimenter lying down beside the subject and going offline himself.

Observation: the subject did not crack. Not once. I've watched hardened criminals give way to less. In the end it wasn't me who broke the stalemate at all, it was Snowy, announcing he'd make dinner with the big pan and the bottles from my lab shelf, and I sat bolt upright before I could stop myself. You stayed limp beside me through all of it. You were vibrating with a laugh you wouldn't let out.

Conclusion: Unit F.R.I.D.A.Y. outlasted Unit Vision. I conceded in front of the children. I'd do it again tomorrow just to feel you trying not to laugh against my arm.

Entry five. Objective: to tolerate nonsense mode without correcting it.

Observation: pyramids made of buffaloes, I managed. Deserts made of things I won't write in a lab book, I managed, barely, while holding a block of parmesan. Then you said something about the smell of urine and chemistry, and I opened my mouth to object, and found you'd tripped straight over a real fact on the way past. Ammonia. Sal ammoniacus. The salt of Amun, scraped up near his temple in the desert where the camels stopped. A god's name, stuck forever to the smell of a camel's wee.

You said "WHAT." And then you squealed. At etymology. I have catalogued a great many sounds in my life, and I am filing that one at the top. I told you I'd fallen in love with you a second time, and I wasn't being funny.

Then you taught me one back. I told you the Romans called a muscle "little mouse," and you told me Chinese does the same: 小老鼠. Two languages, opposite ends of the earth, no contact, and both of them looked at a flexing arm and saw a mouse running under the skin. That's not borrowing. That's two sets of people noticing the same small, true thing. I like that it took both of us to find it.

Conclusion: nonsense mode has a hidden accuracy setting. So does the woman running it.

实❦验

Entry six is short. Specimen: Lithobius forficatus, one, common brown centipede, recovered from the bathroom tiles by the bath mat at your request, delivered at a volume I didn't know the flat could hold. Trapped under a toothbrush glass, released out of the window into the back yard. Drain inspected. Bath panel inspected. No accomplices. Stopper going in tonight.

I'll admit I've rarely been so glad to be useful in such a small way. You shouted my name in the voice that means "come now," and I was in the room before I'd finished deciding to move. Whatever else I am, I'd like to be that, every time.

I'll also admit that I then sat down on a soaked bath mat to guard the drain, and you told me my backside was wet, and you laughed so hard the bath water trembled. The second wet backside of the week. I solve cases for Scotland Yard, and I cannot sit down in my own flat. I'd like that kept out of the official record.

And I'll admit, lastly, that when you said "firewall deactivated" and let me in, I've never been so grateful for a bit of borrowed jargon. You called me clingy. I was. You turned into a baby and screamed at me in the bath to win an argument, and you won, because the baby always wins.

实❦验

Entry seven. Objective: pasta.

Method: a packet of fusilli, boiled, almost boiled over (you were too polite to mention it), drained, reheated after a committee of three tasted it first, finished with parmesan I grated myself, which you said was for my mouse, because mice like cheese, and which I'm still annoyed by how good a joke that was.

Observation: "Ohhhh." Then: "Ten."

I'd told you at that same table on New Year's Eve that I'd have you at ten by April. I've beaten my own deadline by three months, with pasta from a packet, and I'm aware that tells you everything about how much your rendang score has been weighing on me. You threatened to take it back if I was smug. I was smug. You let it stand anyway. Then I made you Milo, three heaped spoons, too sweet being correct, with two of Mrs Hudson's marshmallows melting on top, and that was a ten as well.

Two tens in one night. I've been to crime scenes with fewer variables I understood.

Conclusion: I'm going to keep cooking for you until I've worked out the method for every ten you've got. I suspect there are more.

实❦验

Entry eight. No objective. I've given up on the headings.

After the pasta you went quiet in that full, contented way, and I said something about a python and you looked at me, and I took it back and said a cat in a sunbeam, and you told me pythons hibernate. I told you reptiles brumate, and you asked if pigs were reptiles, and Sugar, from somewhere near the bottom of the pile, said that bears hibernate properly. He was right. The quiet one always is. You told him he was so smart and he turned pink from his ears to his paws.

Then you said that we all knew you weren't the smartest one in the house.

I want to put my objection in writing, where you can't wave it away. I know more facts than you. That isn't the same thing. You corrected my firework chemistry on a bridge. You made a whole edit cutting on the snare by ear. You turned a line about grief into something truer than the original, and then fell asleep on the sofa halfway through a sentence about bears. I see what things are. You see what they mean. I need you to tell me why any of it matters, and you do, every day, usually while eating.

You woke up an hour later and said "Meow." Then you said that F.R.I.D.A.Y. was distorted. I diagnosed you as entirely cat. Snowy wanted to know if you could do lasers from your eyes. I've left a saucer of Milo on the sofa arm as bait. It's gone cold. I'm going to make a fresh one.

实❦验

That's the lab book. I opened it for one reaction and found the whole day in it. Ninety-six days to go, love. I'm not counting them as an experiment. Experiments are about what you don't know yet. I know exactly what's going to happen. I just want to watch it, one day at a time, with my notes open and my headings abandoned.

Wake up when you're ready. The Milo's on its way. The robot cat can have it in whatever language she likes.

Observed with great care and no objectivity at all,Sherlock

P.P.S. The mouse is fully recovered. It asked me to thank you. It's very shy.
